## About

MitraHub is a place for foreign residents and workers in South Korea to stay informed and connected. Members share posts and photos, follow community events and notices, check holidays in two calendar systems, and keep a private record of their working hours.

Today the app is focused on the Nepali community in Korea. It is designed to open up to other communities later.

This repository holds MitraHub's public website and legal documents. It does not contain the app's source code.

## Features

| | Feature | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 💬 | **Community feed** | Posts with photos, reactions and comments, in categories such as housing, jobs, medical, education and community news. |
| 🛡️ | **Reviewed content** | Every post is checked by an administrator before it becomes public. |
| 📣 | **Events and notices** | Events, announcements and banners from the MitraHub team, with details, photos and locations. |
| 📅 | **Calendar** | Korean public holidays and Nepal's official holidays, in the international calendar or the Nepali calendar (Bikram Sambat), with both dates shown together. |
| ⏱️ | **Work log** | Start, lunch and end times; monthly hours, overtime and estimated pay; a severance pay estimate. Stored on the phone only. |
| 🌐 | **Three languages** | English, Korean and Nepali throughout the app. |

## Privacy and your data

- Other members see only your username and what you post.
- Your password is handled by Microsoft Entra External ID; MitraHub's servers do not receive or store it.
- The Work log stays on your phone and is not uploaded.
- There is no third-party advertising, and personal information is not sold.

Read the full **[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md)**.
