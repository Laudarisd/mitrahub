What you can do

Built for daily life as a foreign resident or worker in Korea.

Community feed

Share posts and photos, react and comment. Categories cover housing, jobs, companies, medical, education and community news.

Reviewed before it is public

Every post is checked by an administrator before it appears in the feed, to keep scams and abuse out.

Events and notices

See events, announcements and banners published by the MitraHub team, with details, photos and locations.

Calendar in two systems

Korean public holidays and Nepal's official holidays, shown in the international calendar or the Nepali calendar (Bikram Sambat) with both dates side by side.

Work log

Record start, lunch and end times. See monthly hours, overtime and estimated pay, and a severance pay estimate. Kept on your phone only.

Three languages

Use the whole app in English, Korean or Nepali, and switch at any time.

Who it is for

Today MitraHub is focused on the Nepali community in Korea. It is designed to open up to other communities later, and already works fully in three languages.

You need to be 18 or older and have an email address to create an account.

Your information

  • Other members see only your username and what you post.
  • Your Work log stays on your phone and is not uploaded.
  • No third-party advertising, and personal information is not sold.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time.
