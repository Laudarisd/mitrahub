# MitraHub Privacy Policy

_Effective: 22 November 2026_

This policy explains what the MitraHub app collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Who we are

MitraHub is a community app for people living and working in South Korea. This policy explains what information the MitraHub app collects, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

If you have a question about this policy or about your information, contact the MitraHub team at CONTACT_EMAIL.

## 2. Information you give us

When you create an account or edit your profile, we collect:

- your email address;
- a username, which other members see;
- your first and last name;
- your nationality;
- your phone number;
- your gender and age, only if you choose to give them.

When you use the community features, we collect the posts, photos, comments and reactions you create, and the time you create them.

## 3. Your password

Sign-in is handled by Microsoft Entra External ID. Your password is sent from the app directly to Microsoft, which stores it in hashed form. The MitraHub servers do not receive or store your password. If you choose Google sign-in, Google confirms your identity to Microsoft and MitraHub does not receive your Google password.

## 4. Information collected automatically

To operate the service we keep:

- sign-in records;
- your app language;
- server logs of requests made to the service, which are used to keep it running and to investigate problems.

The calendar reads the country of your mobile network on your phone to choose which public holidays to show first. Only the two-letter country code and the year are sent to our server for this purpose.

## 5. Information that stays on your phone

The Work log (working hours, pay settings and the severance estimate) is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to MitraHub's servers. It is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Your choices about hiding announcement popups and your app theme are also kept on your phone only.

## 6. What we do not collect

MitraHub does not collect your precise location, your contacts, your advertising ID, or the photos on your phone other than the ones you choose to upload. The app shows no third-party advertising and we do not sell personal information.

## 7. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- create and manage your account;
- show your posts, comments and reactions to other members;
- review content before it is published, and act on content or accounts that break the Terms of Use;
- keep one account per phone number;
- respond when you contact us;
- operate, maintain and improve the service.

## 8. Who can see your information

Other members see your username and the content you post. They do not see your name, email address, phone number, nationality, gender or age.

Administrators of MitraHub can see your full profile details when they review content and manage accounts.

We use Microsoft Azure to host the service, store its data and handle sign-in. Information is stored in Microsoft data centres in Japan. To show public holidays, our server requests them from a public holiday data service using only a year and a country code; nothing about you is included.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

## 9. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, comments and uploaded images are permanently removed from the service immediately, and your sign-in record at Microsoft Entra External ID is removed within 30 days. We keep a record that the deletion was requested, containing only the account's internal ID and the time. Copies may remain in backups for a limited period, and content other people have already shared or copied outside MitraHub is outside our control.

## 10. Your choices

You can:

- view and correct your profile details in Edit Profile;
- change your password in Edit Profile;
- edit a post while it is waiting for review, and delete your own posts at any time;
- delete your account and its data at any time, as described on the account deletion page;
- contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL to ask about the information we hold about you.

## 11. Security

Information travels between the app and the service over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and access to each account's data is checked on the server. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Age

MitraHub is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make important changes we will tell you in the app and update the date at the top of this page.

## 14. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy or your information: CONTACT_EMAIL.
