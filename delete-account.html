Delete your account

Effective: 22 November 2026

How to delete your MitraHub account and the data that goes with it.

1. Delete your account in the app

You can delete your MitraHub account yourself at any time:

  • open MitraHub and sign in;
  • tap the menu (the three lines at the top right) and choose Edit Profile;
  • scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account;
  • confirm when asked.

Your profile and everything you posted are removed immediately. This cannot be undone.

2. What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your profile: email address, username, name, nationality, phone number, and gender and age if you gave them;
  • every post you created, with its photos;
  • every comment and reaction you made.

Your sign-in record (your email address and your password, which is held in hashed form by Microsoft Entra External ID) is then removed by the MitraHub team within 30 days of your request. After that the email address can be used to register again.

3. What may remain

We keep a record that the deletion was requested, containing only the account's internal ID and the time, so that we can show the request was carried out.

Copies of deleted data may remain in backups for a limited period before they are overwritten. Content that other people have already shared or copied outside MitraHub is outside our control.

The Work log is stored only on your phone and is not part of your account on our servers. To remove it, uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

4. If you cannot use the app

If you cannot sign in or no longer have the app, email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address your account is registered with and ask for your account to be deleted. We will confirm when it has been done.
